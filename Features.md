### Category 1: Text & Data Analysis (Foundational)
*These tools help users break down and understand the content of a single text.*

- [x] **Keyword Analysis & Word Clouds:** Automatically identify the most frequently used words and concepts in an article. We could display this as a dynamic word cloud, where clicking a word highlights its occurrences in the text.
- [x] **Concordance (Keywords in Context):** Allow a user to click any significant word (e.g., "alienation") and see a list of every single sentence where that word appears. This is incredibly powerful for deep textual analysis.
- [ ] **Named-Entity Recognition (NER):** Automatically find and tag all mentions of people (e.g., "Karl Marx"), places ("Paris"), and organizations. These could be linked to a glossary or other relevant materials.

### Category 2: Connection & Citation Analysis (Advanced)
*These tools focus on how articles relate to each other, building a web of knowledge.*

- [ ] **Citation Network Visualization:** Like the popular tool `Connected Papers`, we can create a visual graph showing which articles in your library cite the current one, and which articles it cites. This is a fantastic tool for discovering new and relevant literature.
- [ ] **"Smart Citations":** Instead of just listing a reference, we could analyze *how* it's being cited. For example, does the author support, critique, or merely mention the cited work? This provides critical context.
- [ ] **Reference Linking:** Automatically turn bibliographic references into live links, either to other articles within your platform or to external sources.

### Category 3: AI-Powered Reading Assistance (Cutting-Edge)
*These features use AI to make complex texts more accessible.*

- [ ] **"Explain This" Function:** Allow users to highlight a difficult sentence or paragraph and have an AI provide a simplified explanation or define the key terms. This would be a game-changer for dense theoretical or scientific texts.
- [ ] **AI-Generated Summaries:** Create a button that generates a concise summary of the entire article or a selected section.
Argument & Thesis Detection: An AI feature that attempts to automatically identify and extract the core thesis and supporting arguments of a paper.
